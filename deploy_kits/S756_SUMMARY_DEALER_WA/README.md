## What it does
1. **The dealer reminder.** From the 7th of the month after an ended counted month (`summary_wa.from_day`, 7), at or after 11:30 IST (`summary_wa.hhmm`), **every day, Sundays too**, one WhatsApp per awaited dealer per day from the reception phone, on the same road as the NEFT messages (`supplier_msg`, kind `summary`). The words are the owner's, the month in Hindi:
   *संजीवनी मेडिकोज़, बरेली: अक्टूबर 2026 के बिलों की समरी अभी तक हमें नहीं मिली है। समय से पेमेंट के लिए शीघ्र भेज दें, धन्यवाद।*
   It goes to the dealer's **payment-message** number in the phone book. No number: nothing is sent, the row is `skipped · number nahi`, and the snapshot says **number missing**. It stops by itself when the summary is recognised, when the owner marks *gives no summary*, or when he pauses the dealer. An unsent message is withdrawn when the day turns, so a phone that was off never sends a backlog. **Fails closed:** if `assets.db` cannot be read, nothing is queued. A locked month and the parked September are never chased. Written only by owner_notice's five-minute tick (`scan_register.s756_queue`); `summary_wa.on = off` stops it.
2. **The snapshot** at the top of an ended month's purchase check (`/finance/purchase/page/check?month=2026-10`): the counts; the awaited dealers with bills, Marg ₹, days waiting, the last reminder and **Pause reminders / Resume** (the doctor only; door `/finance/purchase/api/s756/pause`, audited); the differences that are his (Ask the dealer / Accept, the row-7 doors); the rest folded; the parked months named as not chased. Read only.
3. **Row 7 (F-1208):** the count line names every dealer (agree · differs · explained · settled · your confirm · give no summary · unread · awaited); a parked month's awaited line reads *Parked month · nobody is asked and nobody is reminded*; a counted month's adds *the dealer is reminded on WhatsApp each day from 07-Nov*.
4. **F-1207:** `owner_notice.py`'s `if __name__ == "__main__"` guard is moved to the END of the file. S755's section had been appended after it, so cron's `owner_notice.py tick` exited before S755 was defined: **the morning summaries line to the owner and Shavez never ran.** It runs from the first tick after this install. S755's own cron check imported the module (never ran the file) and so could not see it; `check_s756_cron.py` runs the file. The morning line also loses Marg's padding in the dealers' names and says nothing if `assets.db` cannot be read.
