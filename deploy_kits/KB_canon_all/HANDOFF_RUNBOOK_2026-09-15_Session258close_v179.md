## §1 · MENTAL MODELS — what to hold in your head next session

**1 · The nightly owns four things, and the close VERIFIES them.** `NIGHTLY.bat` at 03:10 runs the
Cowork manifest rebuild, the maintenance pass and the canon gate, and leaves three reports. **A
missing, stale or non-zero report is a RED — not a blank — and the close then does that step by
hand.** Automation removed the labour, not the check.

**2 · manojz is a personal PC and may be off at 03:10.** The task now catches up the next time the
machine is awake, so **a report may legitimately carry a morning clock time. Read the time, not the
date.**

**3 · The live server's own code is readable.** `D:\Downloads\_kbtools\vps_code\code_nightly.tar.gz`,
refreshed nightly, self-verifying. Therefore: **no patcher is built from an inferred anchor when the
real file can be read, and no pin is predicted when the real file can be hashed.**

**4 · Each backup is correct about what it excludes; the gap lives between them.** That is why six
live files had no copy anywhere while no single store was misconfigured. The bundle and the pin list
can now be held against each other — **do it whenever a new live file is pinned.**

**5 · Two ways to be wrong that cost this session twice.**
*A page read as text is not the data* — a status chip ran into a vendor's name and became a finding.
*A store is not checked until every one of its inclusion lists is read* — one list of two produced a
false gap. **A false gap costs what a missed one does.**

**6 · Every scheduled job on all three machines can now be stopped by a file.** A kit that changes a
guarded job may switch it off first. **`ALL_OFF` on the medical PC deliberately does not stop
capture** — Marg reuses one filename, so an export not captured in that instant is gone for ever.

**7 · Do not hand the owner a step that is not his.** His ruling, and rule 11 of the standing set.
A maintenance step goes into the nightly, or the assistant performs it. An installer reaches him
only for a credential, a GUI action, a decision, or a command on the VPS.

**8 · A number that improved is checked harder than one that got worse.** F-491: the paper shelf's
never-cited figure went 51 % → 0 % in one night and read as total success. It was an artefact of a
different tool doing its job correctly. **A metric whose corpus is a machine-generated listing of
everything measures nothing** — and this class of fault arrives disguised as good news, with no error
anywhere, which is exactly why A0's rule exists.

**9 · Count the set; do not sample it.** F-492: four kits carried `KIT_ID` + `README` + `SUMS` and
the fifth carried none, and nobody looked because four out of four is a persuasive argument about the
fifth. **Consistency is an expectation, not evidence.** *No kit is published until it carries its own
three, and the close counts them per kit.*
