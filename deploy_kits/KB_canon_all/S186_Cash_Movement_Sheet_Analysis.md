## What was WRONG here

~~"The 13 Aug ₹75,000 was banked by Dr Bhawna."~~ — **No.** The owner confirmed it stayed in her
custody, and the Yes Bank statement then showed **no August entries at all**. The 13 Aug deposit
never happened: that is **F-112**, a phantom deposit in the live books.

~~"Dr Bhawna deposits to the bank directly — that explains the missing April return leg."~~ — **No.**
The owner: *"dr bhawna never banks, all sanjeevni bank deposits are by darpan only."* The April
return leg is simply unrecorded; the cash went back to Darpan and he banked it.

**The lesson worth keeping.** Both errors came from the same move: an arithmetic gap appeared, a
mechanism was invented that would close it neatly, and the neatness was mistaken for evidence. Both
were killed within minutes by one sentence from the person who runs the process. The float was
eventually settled not by any derivation but by **counting the notes** (D323).

## Where the numbers went

Each correction pushed the implied float up — ₹1,47,207 → ₹1,54,516 → ₹1,71,721 — because every
discovery added cash to Dr Bhawna's side and none took any away. That drift was itself the signal
that the sheet records inflows to her more completely than outflows from her, and that no
reconstruction from it would converge. **Final answer: ₹87,205, parked by count.**
