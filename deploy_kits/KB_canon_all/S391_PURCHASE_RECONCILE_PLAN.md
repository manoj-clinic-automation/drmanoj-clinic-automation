|---|---|---|
| **Reception** | every evening | scan the day's bills; their list carries only: *Scan karo* (a Marg bill with no scan, days waited), *Dobara scan karo*, and one-tap questions (*Kya yeh wahi bill hai?* where only the amount pairs). |
