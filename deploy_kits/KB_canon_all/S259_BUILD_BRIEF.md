## THE FOUR THINGS WORTH KNOWING

### 1 · Your money is not leaking, and here is the proof

You told me Darpan marks UPI and cash wrongly in the end-of-day rush, works from the POS summary and
the day's total, and must not be given anything more to do at that hour. Tested your way — **on the
total, not the split** — every September day the bank took **more** UPI than Marg recorded and never
once less, which is exactly that mis-marking. And across **611 payments since June there is not one
day on which the bank took more than the whole day's sale**, which is where a real loss would show.

**The check itself is what is wrong.** It compares a split you have told me is unreliable by design.
It gets replaced with the total, and **the right check already exists and has never carried a single
day**: sale, minus home medicines, minus procedure medicines, minus online, equals the cash that
should have been handed over. It needs a place in your portal and your three stock corrections —
nothing is asked of Darpan at night.

### 2 · Nothing on your server is dead

Of thirty-eight scheduled jobs, only eight could prove they had run — **and all five of your nightly
backups were among the thirty that could not.** Your health page has a check meant to watch those
backups, and that check is one of seven that have never spoken in a fortnight. So the thing that
would save the clinic left no proof, the watchman over it had never reported, and neither state could
be told from the other.

It now can. **35 alive, one quiet by design, one belonging to another app, and one — your
asset-register backup — that names no log and throws its errors away.** That last one I will fix.

### 3 · I was wrong four times in one day

Fourteen vendors needing bank details were **three**. A certificate watch I called silent was doing
exactly what a healthy watch does. A tool of mine reported a job as having run *in the future* and
called it healthy. And a job that runs every five minutes read as dead because it prints nothing when
there is nothing to say.

**Every one came from giving you a number without first asking what would make it wrong.** The rules
against that already existed, so another rule was not the answer. The habit is: name the thing that
would falsify the number, and go and look at that before speaking. It is why the vendor list was
corrected before you acted on it, and why I never told you ₹12,322 had gone missing.

### 4 · Two small things found while looking elsewhere

**Bill 160 of 1 September exists twice** — two suppliers, same number, same day — so five lines worth
**₹17,777** sit in September's total and on no vendor's payment sheet. Once in 1,551 lines. It does
not touch August, so your close is unaffected. Mine to fix.

**And the vendor list you asked for is three names**, one of which — KUSHAGRA MEDICAL AGENCY — was on
no list of yours.
