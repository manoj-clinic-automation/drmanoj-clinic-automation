## 2 · The four parts

**2.1 Pairing by the scanned bill (his 22-Nov rule: the scanned bill over reception's taps, everywhere).** In `porders.detect_supplies` and `stock_figure.carried`:
- (new rule 0, before a–e) an order line whose order has a tied scan **linked to a Marg bill** (purchase_scan_link → purchase_bill) is carried by THAT bill's matching line and by no other; a bill so taken never carries another line of the same item (rule b already enforces it per line).
- a line with `missing=1` and `supplied` 0/NULL **never takes a bill** (it never came).
- a second tap of the same delivery (same item, same dealer, an earlier tap within arrival.back_days already carried by the same bill, no second Marg bill) is **named a duplicate tap** — never a delivery, never counted as arrived; the owner's Needs-you shows it once with "Reception tapped this twice".
- **Re-pair once, guarded** (a one-off in the installer, its own walk on a copy, control red): every answered line whose own scan link names another bill is re-answered from the scan; `billed_bill_no` / `billed_qty` / `billed_date` rewritten with an audit row each (`line_repaired_s952`, old → new); lines no rule can answer are listed, not guessed.

**2.2 Every count judged against the one record.** `stock_watch._s934_settle`, `write_point`, `settle_provisional` (and `open_trace`'s E) take the expected figure from `stock_record` at the count's moment, not from `stock_figure.shelf`:
- **expected = Marg's figure at the point** (the record's MARG side: the closing standing for the end of the day before + Amir's bills keyed late + the spine's moves up to the moment; for a bill-placed count, the count day's sales up to its bill) **+ the record's WAITING lines that are truly waiting at that moment** (scan_not_in_marg / tap_no_scan; a carried or duplicate line never).
- Every difference carries **its cause in plain words** on the Stock check page (ruling 22-Nov): "matches Marg"; "below Marg by x — a loss since the last count"; "below Marg by exactly a delivery — that delivery may not have been on the shelf (bill …)"; "Marg still holds an older loss of dd-mm not yet written off (Amir's voucher pending) — not a new loss" (the record's history of booked losses decides this — a loss is booked once, never twice).
