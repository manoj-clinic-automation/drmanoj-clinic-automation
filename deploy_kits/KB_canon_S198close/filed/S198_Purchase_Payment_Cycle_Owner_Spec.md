## The sequence, start of each new month

1. **Bill-wise purchase report** generated (Marg).
2. **Manual verification against the physical bills**; any corrections are made **in Marg
   purchases**.
3. **Supplier-wise purchase report** generated (after corrections).
4. **Physical verification against the vendors' month summaries** — usually **2 summaries per
   vendor per month**; each carries a copy of the staff-signed receiving bill + a summary page
   on top.
5. After reconciliation, **Amir makes his own Excel** *(owner: "which I think is not a proper
   one")* and hands a **printout with his signature**. Amir's sheet keeps **2 columns per
   vendor** (one per summary) **plus any carry-forward debit/credit**.
6. **Darpan signs** all the summaries and Amir's sheet.
7. **Shavez makes the NEFT sheet + the cheque + the letter; the owner signs.**
8. Physical copies delivered to the bank **AND a bank-required email is sent with the same
   NEFT excel attached, FROM `sanjeevni.bly@gmail.com`** ⚠ *(an account NEW to the canon —
   not in the estate inventory; add at the next estate reconcile)*. **Only after that email
   does the bank execute.**
9. After payment, **Amir reconciles the payment in Marg**.
10. A copy of the NEFT sheet is **physically filed**.
