How to read it. Total sale is the day's net sale as filed from Marg (cash + UPI lines, returns netted). UPI is the bank's settled figure once its statement is in. Cash = total sale − UPI. Home medicine and procedure medicine are bills raised with no cash across the counter (Darpan's labels in Marg), so they come off the cash; adjustment is a home / procedure return put back (goods returned, no cash). Paid at clinic is a pharmacy bill whose money was taken at the clinic counter (the owner's ruling, e.g. 20-Aug bill 2777) -- it is income, not a bill without cash. Net cash is the month's cash income; Income adds the UPI to it; handed is what left Darpan's drawer: to Dr Manoj or Dr Bhawna (as recorded in August), to the pool the two of you share (every day you approve -- your ruling of 21-Sep), and to the bank (the pool's deposits included).
