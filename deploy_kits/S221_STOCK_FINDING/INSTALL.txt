ever seen a real count, and it stays unproven until one happens.

The patcher refuses and changes nothing unless all four anchors match exactly once, keeps a
timestamped .bak, and restores it if the result will not compile. Re-running says "already
patched". The four new tables are created lazily on first use; no migration step.
