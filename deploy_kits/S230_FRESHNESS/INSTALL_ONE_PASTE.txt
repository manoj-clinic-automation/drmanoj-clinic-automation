S230_FRESHNESS — install, ONE LINE AT A TIME.

Nothing here is destructive. The script is strictly READ-ONLY against everything it
watches: it opens databases in read-only mode, stats files, reads logs. It never
writes to, moves, restarts or signals any other service. The only files it creates
are its own four: a page, a JSON, a state file and its own log.

You never type a secret in this install. Step 6 copies the phone-alert topic that is
already on the box.

STOP AT STEP 12 and send me what it printed. Steps 14 onward come after that.
