WHY THE PROVEN S200 PATTERN DID NOT TRANSFER, and this is the finding. S200 brought the staff
register in with ONE LiteSpeed context because that app's routes were already namespaced
(/register/...). The scan app's routes are FLAT: /, /login, /intake, /bills, /assets, /files,
/scan, /drafts, /staff, /vendors, /renewals, /purchases, /admin, /account, /api/due. Ten route
families. One context cannot cover them; ten would claim ten top-level paths on the portal's own
domain — including /assets, which means a different thing to each app. So the APP learns the
prefix instead, in nine lines of WSGI.

WHAT IT DOES. One process, both shapes. assets.dr-manoj.in keeps working exactly as today; the
same app also answers under /scanapp, and Flask's url_for/redirects come back prefixed on their
own. Nothing moves. No port changes. The subdomain is not retired.
