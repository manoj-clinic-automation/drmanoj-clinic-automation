**What changes on the staff Call Console** (`/portal/console/staff`). Every missed call of **yesterday** that is still **unresolved** is on today's lists, on the missed-call path. Unresolved = no connected call after it (that day or today), no WhatsApp from him after it (the WhatsApp tab has him then), and **no Docterz visit from yesterday on** (finance.db `patient_visit`, read-only — by the clinic ID, or by the calling mobile's fingerprint, so a patient on another mobile is caught once Docterz holds that mobile; a visit shows only once its export reached the server). Today is a fresh day for tries: none → **Incoming missed** (lit, before today's, oldest first), one → **Call again**, two → **Evening calls**, a third ends it; yesterday's tries are said on the line ("tried 3 times yesterday"). The line reads **"Missed after hours (21:40)"** when every open miss was from 21:00 on (setting `PORTAL_STAFF_AFTER_HOURS_FROM`, default `21:00`), else **"Missed yesterday, not reached (14:10)"**; a new number adds **"new number - may be a patient on another mobile"**. A number that also missed today shows once, on today's line; a follow-up patient shows once, on the missed path. Nothing else moves: the follow-up path, WhatsApp, Handled today, his own page.

**Decisions made in the build (stated, not asked):** only yesterday is read (a miss two days old is not carried); spam and Not-a-patient numbers are never carried; if finance.db cannot be read, or yesterday's Docterz export is not in yet, the calls still carry and the Incoming missed tab says "Docterz visits are not up to date - a caller from yesterday may already have come. Check before calling." (`carry_visits_ok: false`); a fault reading yesterday costs the carry only and the tab says "Yesterday's missed calls could not be read just now - try again in a few minutes." (`carry_ok: false`), never the page. A patient who walks in today leaves the list when tonight's export arrives. The fuller flow for new numbers (naming the caller from the call verdict) is a separate kit, its screen shown to him first.
