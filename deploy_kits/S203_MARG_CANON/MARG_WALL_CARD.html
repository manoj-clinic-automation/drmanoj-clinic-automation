Generating the report

On the MEDICAL PC: Marg → Daily Reports → Sale Reports → BILL WISE STATEMENT

  • Report Type = Detail  (Summary-1 loses the CASH column)
  • With Item Deta. = Yes
  • Day Total = Yes · Less Cr/Dr Adj. = Yes
  • Report For = 2 Sale-S/R-Brk · 80 Col · Disc.Bill Sign = 2-Bill+Item
  • Operator / mobiles blank · Single Party, Group, COMPNA = N

Export screen: separator Header · heading No/1 · data from line 5 · ends 0 · Formated

ONE DAY AT A TIME. Month-to-date with item detail once stopped at day 6 of 15 and said nothing. One business day per export.

Backup — the crown jewels

  • Take a Marg backup every 2–3 days. It lands on E:\.
  • The agent copies it off-site to Drive by itself, within the hour.
  • The heartbeat shouts after 3 days without one.
  • Back up last financial year too — it is rarely done.
A RESTORE MUST GO INTO A NEW OR TEST COMPANY. Never over the live one. If the engineer says it can only restore over the live company — stop and ask first.
