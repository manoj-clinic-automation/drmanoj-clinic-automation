WHAT IT DOES
  One table, one authority. Seeded from stock_app._voucher_section()'s OWN rule reduced to the item
  name — consumable first, then orthotic, else medicine — because that is already the rule that
  prints the section heading on a Marg voucher in front of Amir. Seeding is INSERT-ONLY and never
  overwrites the owner's word; what the seed had said is kept beside his, so the disagreement stays
  visible instead of being tidied away. The page shows every disputed item, WHY it is disputed, and
  three taps.

WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO
  It changes nothing. The four word lists stay where they are and keep doing their own jobs. No
  screen, no figure and no decision reads stock_item_section. That is the point of building this
  rung first: the seed may be wrong for a while and it must cost nothing while it is. The word lists
  are COPIED into section_map.py rather than imported, so this table cannot silently re-classify
  itself because somebody adds a word to a pricing list months later.
