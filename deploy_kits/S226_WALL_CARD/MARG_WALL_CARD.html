Generating the report

On the MEDICAL PC: Marg → Daily Reports → Sale Reports → BILL WISE STATEMENT

  • Report Type = Detail  (Summary-1 loses the CASH column)
  • With Item Deta. = Yes
  • Day Total = Yes · Less Cr/Dr Adj. = Yes
  • Report For = 2 Sale-S/R-Brk · 80 Col · Disc.Bill Sign = 2-Bill+Item
  • Operator / mobiles blank · Single Party, Group, COMPNA = N

Export screen: separator Header · heading No/1 · data from line 5 · ends 0 · Formated

EVERY MORNING, ONE EXPORT FOR THE LAST TWO DAYS (yesterday and the day before — the owner's default from 06-Sep-2026). A bill entered late for a closed day is caught by the next morning's export; the server keeps the newer export of a day. Never month-to-date with item detail — it once stopped at day 6 of 15 and said nothing.

Closing stock (Marg → Stock Reports → closing stock, whole store): every morning too, after every one of yesterday's bills — sale and purchase — has been entered. It reaches the count page and the drift page within ten minutes on its own. Marg cannot re-issue a closing stock for a past date: the export is always "as of now", so whatever has not been entered by the time you take it is missing from that day for good.

Purchases — ONE report, and mind the name. Marg → Purchase → BILL ITEM WISE, from the last export date to today. That single report is enough: it carries the quantity and each bill's own date, so the stock figure can place every purchase on the right day by itself (06-Sep-2026).

PURCHASE BILL ITEM WISE is not PURCHASE ITEM WISE. They are two different reports one word apart, and the wrong one costs a morning: on 06-Sep the right data was exported three times into a reader that was looking at the other report, and the stock figure sat unchanged at a four-day-old horizon while everybody assumed it was updating.
Enter a bill the day it arrives. A purchase bill entered days later reaches the books only from the day it is entered — and yesterday's closing stock can never be taken again. On 06-Sep six bills dated 05-Sep were entered on the 6th; the 05‑Sep comparison is wrong for ever, and eleven items show a gap that was never on the shelf.

Stock-check morning: enter every pending bill → export BILL ITEM WISE purchases → export closing stock → wait ten minutes → count.

  • Take a Marg backup every 2–3 days. It lands on E:\.
  • The agent copies it off-site to Drive by itself, within the hour.
