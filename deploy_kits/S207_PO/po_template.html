The phone number is asked for once. Whoever first needs to ring a stockist types it in; after that it is a tap to call, for everyone. The number is not held anywhere else — the vendor sheet has never come back — so the page collects it at the moment somebody actually wants it.

No prices on this page, deliberately. Quantities are what the counter and the stockist need; what it costs is Dr Manoj’s business and is not printed where staff work.

How the quantity is worked out. (daily rate × cover) − on hand, rounded up to whole strips. Cover is the vendor’s cadence plus 2 days lead and 3 days safety, with 3 more for an item only one stockist carries. Lead time is still a placeholder until the vendor sheet comes back — it is the last unmeasured number in the model.

What is deliberately not ordered. Anything already above its cover. Anything unsold for 60 days. Any line too small to be worth a handling, unless it is actually out of stock. And nothing where the daily rate comes from too few selling days, or from one big day — those ask instead.
