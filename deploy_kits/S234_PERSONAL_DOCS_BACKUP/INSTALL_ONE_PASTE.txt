S234_PERSONAL_DOCS_BACKUP v1 — install.

WHAT THIS DOES, IN ONE SENTENCE
Your Personal Documents sheet — the one your Janitor keeps identity-document
rows and their expiry dates in — joins the four books already pulled to the VPS
at 01:45 and shipped inside the encrypted nightly bundle at 01:50.

WHY IT MATTERS
It is the last thing in your personal Google account with exactly ONE copy, in
ONE account. Nothing else in the estate is in that position any more.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
It adds no job, no key and no mechanism, and it changes not one line of the
pull or the bundle. It adds one book to a list. Nothing is written to GitHub —
that book and its numbers never enter the repository, and this kit carries no
row of it.

=============================================================================
 THE INSTALL — TWO LINES. Paste the first, wait for it, then paste the second.
=============================================================================

[VPS] 1 · pull the deploy clone:

git -C /root/deploy/repo fetch --depth 1 origin main && git -C /root/deploy/repo reset --hard origin/main

[VPS] 2 · run the installer:

bash /root/deploy/repo/deploy_kits/S234_PERSONAL_DOCS_BACKUP/install.sh

=============================================================================
 THE ONE THING THAT MIGHT ASK SOMETHING OF YOU
=============================================================================

The four books already being pulled were shared with the backup's service
account long ago. This fifth one probably has not been.

If it has not, the installer stops at the preflight stage, PUTS THE SETTINGS
BACK EXACTLY AS THEY WERE, and prints one email address on screen. Open your
Personal Documents sheet in Google, press Share, give that address VIEWER
access, and run the second line again. Nothing else is needed and nothing is
lost in between — tonight's backup runs exactly as last night's either way.

The address is not written here on purpose: the installer reads it off the box,
so it is always the right one.

=============================================================================
 WHERE THE NUMBERS END UP — said plainly, because they are identity documents
=============================================================================

* as plain CSV at /root/state_backup/sheets/personal_docs/ on the VPS — which
  is inside D429, your own ruling that the VPS is a safe place for your data,
  on a box only you hold credentials for;
* inside the AES-256 bundle on the clinic Drive, never in plain form there;
* nowhere else. Not in GitHub, not on any PC, not in this kit.

It is a real second copy and not a pretend one: the book lives in your PERSONAL
account and the bundle lands in the CLINIC account, encrypted on the way.
