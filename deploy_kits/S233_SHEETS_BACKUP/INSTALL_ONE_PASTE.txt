=============================================================================
WHAT TO EXPECT, SO A NORMAL NIGHT IS NOT MISTAKEN FOR A FAULT
=============================================================================

* exit 0  — every book pulled. The ordinary night.
* exit 41 — PERMISSION. A sheet is genuinely not shared. This one, and ONLY
            this one, means go to Google and share it.
* exit 43 — RATE LIMIT, after retrying. THE SHARING IS FINE. Do not re-share
            anything. Either raise API_MIN_INTERVAL_S in the conf or let the
            next night take it. (v1 printed the exit-41 words for this case
            and would have sent you to Google for nothing. That was the bug.)
* exit 42 — a tab emptied, vanished, or lost more than a fifth of its rows.
            The new pull is THROWN AWAY and the previous export still ships.
            Look at the sheet: the sheet is what changed, not the backup.

None of 41, 42 or 43 stops the 01:50 bundle. It ships the last good copy on
disk and records its age in _TAKEN_AT.json.
