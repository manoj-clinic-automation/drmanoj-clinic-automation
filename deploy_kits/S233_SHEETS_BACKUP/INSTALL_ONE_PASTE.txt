S233_SHEETS_BACKUP v1 — install, ONE LINE AT A TIME. After PUBLISH + deploy pull.

WHAT THIS KIT DOES, IN ONE SENTENCE: the live Google Sheets — the only data in
this estate with zero copies anywhere — start riding inside the AES-256 nightly
bundle that has shipped to Drive since S230 and whose restore has been drilled.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO: it does not create a second backup mechanism, a second key,
a second destination or a second cron destination. One new script that writes
CSV to a directory, and ONE new line in the job that already works.

=============================================================================
NOTHING TO SUBSTITUTE. NO KEY. NO SECRET. Two owner actions, and step 8 tells
you if the second is even needed.
=============================================================================

OWNER ACTION A — none before step 1. Start pasting.

OWNER ACTION B — ONLY IF step 8 names a sheet as NOT SHARED. Open that sheet in
Google, Share, paste the service account's address (step 7 prints it), give it
"Viewer", untick "Notify people", Send. Then run step 8 again. A sheet the
service account cannot read cannot be backed up, and this kit refuses to
pretend otherwise.

=============================================================================
WHAT TO EXPECT, SO A NORMAL NIGHT IS NOT MISTAKEN FOR A FAULT
=============================================================================

* exit 0  — every book pulled. The ordinary night.
* exit 41 — a book could not be opened. Its LAST GOOD export is untouched and
            still ships. Usually a share was withdrawn, or a sheet was renamed
            away. The log names the book.
* exit 42 — a tab lost more than a fifth of its rows, or emptied, or vanished.
            The new pull is THROWN AWAY and the previous export still ships.
            This is the guard working, not a failure of the backup. Look at the
            sheet before doing anything: the sheet is the thing that changed.

An exit 41 or 42 does NOT stop the 01:50 bundle. The bundle ships whatever good
copy is on disk and records its age in _TAKEN_AT.json.
