## 0. Vision & Guiding Principles

**Vision.** A bilingual (English + Hindi) library of accurate, reassuring, lay-friendly orthopedic education that (a) genuinely empowers ordinary people — *सेवा में सुख* — (b) deepens patient trust and authority, (c) drives organic website traffic, and (d) feeds an engagement loop across the website, patient app, EMR, and WhatsApp, with the co-located lab as a natural diagnostic funnel. Purpose and practice pulling in the same direction.

**Guiding principles** (apply to every page):

1. **Clinically accurate** — content the surgeon can stand behind; honest evidence grading (nutraceuticals, proximal fibular osteotomy, body-fat estimates etc. are not oversold).
2. **Reassurance-led** — conservative-first; a diagnosis is a reason to act early, not to fear.
3. **Empowerment, not fear** — especially on safety and metabolic topics; never alarmist, never anti-doctor.
4. **Educator, not treater, outside core scope** — for non-orthopedic topics (e.g. diabetes/hypertension awareness) content is pure patient empowerment with firm "see your treating physician" framing. *Dr. Agarwal does not and will not treat these conditions; the content is health-literacy service only.*
5. **NMC-compliant** — patient-education framing only; no guarantees, superlatives, comparative/"best" claims, or testimonials-as-outcomes.
6. **E-E-A-T** — visible author/medical-review credentials + references on every medical page.
7. **Facilities awareness, factually** — the clinic's strong stack is surfaced where clinically relevant, never as advertising (see §9).
8. **Bilingual by design** — English primary for SEO, Hindi for reach.
9. **Phased & sustainable** — no deadline; build cluster by cluster; no hub goes live thin.

## 3. Blog Pages — Content Specifications

Compact spec per page: audience · primary intent · key clinical points · cross-links · figures · video · facility/lab hook.

### PILLAR 1 — Joint & Spine Pain

#### 1.1 Knee Osteoarthritis *(prototype — build first)*
- **Audience/intent:** adults 45+ · "knee osteoarthritis / knee pain treatment"
- **Key points:** cartilage; **not just wear & tear** (mechanical + metabolic + low-grade inflammation); risk factors (age, post-menopausal women, **weight = key modifiable**, prior injury, occupational squatting, family history); symptoms (activity pain, brief AM stiffness, crepitus, intermittent swelling, phasic); **"not every knee is OA"** differentials (gout/hyperuricemia, pseudogout/CPPD, RA, spondyloarthritis, **septic = same-day emergency**); diagnosis (clinical + **weight-bearing X-ray**, MRI not routine, bloods if inflammatory); treatment ladder — **physiotherapy & exercise (foundation:** quads/hip strengthening, ROM, low-impact conditioning, modalities as adjuncts, gait/footwear/bracing, supervised→home, prehab/post-op; bust "use wears it out" myth); weight management; **anti-inflammatory lifestyle** (sleep, stress, diet/gut, omega-3); **nutraceuticals honestly graded** (curcumin/haldi — bioavailability caveat; boswellia/shallaki; moringa — thin OA evidence; supplement-safety caveat for DM/HTN/CKD/blood thinners; adjuncts not replacements); medications (flare bridges); injections; **joint-preserving surgery** (HTO; proximal fibular osteotomy/resection — emerging/selective/honest); → UKR → TKR (last step)
- **Cross-links:** sarcopenia, osteoporosis, vitamin D, self-medication, gout, knee replacement
- **Figures:** healthy vs OA knee · quads exercises · OA-vs-gout-vs-RA · red-flags strip
- **Tool embed:** weight–knee-load visualiser; BMI/WHtR
- **Lab hook:** uric acid / CRP-ESR / Bone & Joint Panel where inflammatory suspected
- **Video:** knee OA (script finalised)

#### 1.2 Low Back Pain
- **Intent:** "low back pain / kamar dard" · mechanical vs serious causes; posture/ergonomics; core + physio; most resolve; **red flags** (bowel/bladder, progressive neuro deficit, fever, trauma, night pain, cancer history); honest imaging stance
- **Cross-links:** osteoporosis (vertebral fracture), sciatica, sarcopenia, axial SpA, self-medication · **Video:** finalised

#### 1.3 Cervical Spondylosis
- **Intent:** "neck pain / cervical spondylosis" · age-related changes; radiculopathy; conservative (traction, nerve gliding, scapular stabilisation), posture/screen ergonomics; **myelopathy red flags** (hand clumsiness, gait/balance, bladder)
- **Cross-links:** low back pain, sciatica, self-medication · **Video:** finalised

#### 1.4 Frozen Shoulder
- **Intent:** "frozen shoulder / kandhe ka dard" · adhesive capsulitis, three phases, **diabetes association**, physio-led, time-course reassurance, injections selected
- **Cross-links:** secondary osteoporosis/diabetes thread, metabolic strand · **Video:** finalised

#### 1.5 Sciatica
- **Intent:** "sciatica" · nerve-root pain, usually disc; conservative; nerve glides; **cauda equina = emergency**; imaging when indicated
- **Cross-links:** low back pain, osteoporosis, self-medication · **Video:** finalised

#### 1.6 Sports Injuries
- **Intent:** "sports injury treatment" · common knee/ankle/shoulder; acute care (PRICE); when surgical; prevention; **post-injury OA risk**
- **Cross-links:** knee OA, physiotherapy · **Video:** finalised

#### 1.7 AVN of the Hip
- **Audience/intent:** young adults 25–45 · "avascular necrosis hip / hip pain young adult"
- **Key points:** osteonecrosis of femoral head — blood supply lost → bone death → collapse; **causes** (steroids, alcohol, trauma, idiopathic, sickle cell); often missed in the young; early groin pain; **diagnosis** (X-ray late vs **MRI early — key**; Ficat/ARCO staging); **early diagnosis is everything** (pre-collapse = joint-preserving e.g. core decompression; post-collapse → THR); treat the cause; durable THR in young when needed
- **Cross-links:** **self-medication/steroids (strong)**, secondary osteoporosis, hip replacement · **Figures:** blood supply · staging · early-MRI message · **Video:** future

#### Inflammatory Arthritis sub-cluster
> **Scoping:** position as **early-recogniser, diagnostician, and appropriate referrer/co-manager** — DMARDs/biologics are rheumatology-led. Role = early detection (X-ray + lab), conservative care, MSK/surgical sequelae.

**1.8 "Wear-and-tear or inflammatory? When joint pain is more than osteoarthritis"** *(the differentiator — high value)*
- **Intent:** "inflammatory arthritis vs osteoarthritis" · inflammatory features (**prolonged AM stiffness >30–60 min**, multiple/symmetric joints, swelling/warmth, systemic symptoms, younger onset, **eases with activity / worse with rest**) vs OA; the **early-treatment window**; tests (RF, anti-CCP, ANA, HLA-B27, uric acid, CRP/ESR); when to seek specialist review
- **Cross-links:** all sub-cluster pages, knee OA, secondary osteoporosis · **Lab hook:** the full inflammatory panel

**1.9 Rheumatoid Arthritis** — symmetric small-joint (hands/wrists/feet), morning stiffness, systemic; RF/anti-CCP; **early DMARDs change outcomes**; deformity if untreated; regular monitoring; refer rheum · cross-links: secondary osteoporosis (RA + steroids), self-medication, lab

**1.10 Axial Spondyloarthritis / Ankylosing Spondylitis** — **inflammatory back pain in the young** (<40, insidious, >3 mo, AM stiffness, improves with exercise, night pain, alternating buttock pain); HLA-B27; enthesitis; extra-articular (uveitis, psoriasis, IBD); exercise + biologics; refer · cross-links: low back pain, lab

**1.11 Psoriatic Arthritis** — psoriasis (skin/nails), asymmetric, dactylitis ("sausage digit"), enthesitis; can precede/follow skin; refer · cross-links: RA, lab

**1.12 Gout & Crystal Arthritis** — hyperuricemia; acute monoarthritis (podagra; knee too), red-hot-swollen sudden/overnight; triggers; chronic tophi; urate-lowering + lifestyle; **CPPD/pseudogout** in elderly (knee/wrist) · cross-links: knee OA, self-medication, metabolic strand, lab (uric acid)

### PILLAR 2 — Bone & Muscle Health

#### 2.1 Osteoporosis
- "silent disease"; fracture risk (hip/spine/wrist); **DEXA screening**; risk factors; calcium + vitamin D; **weight-bearing + resistance exercise**; bone-active medicines (general); fall prevention; **vegetarian calcium** (dairy, ragi, til, greens, soya); India vitamin-D thread
- Cross-links: sarcopenia, falls, vitamin D, fragility fracture, secondary osteoporosis, pregnancy/lactation, decades · Lab hook: Bone & Joint Panel, vit D, calcium, PTH · Video: finalised

#### 2.2 Sarcopenia
- age-related muscle loss; **why it matters** (worsens OA, falls, fractures, surgery recovery); **osteosarcopenia "dangerous duo"**; assessment (strength/mass/performance; DEXA/BIA); management = **progressive resistance training + protein (≈1.0–1.2 g/kg, vegetarian detail) + vitamin D**; simple home strength moves (sit-to-stand, wall push-ups, leg extensions, calf raises, step-ups, bands); start gently/supervised
- Cross-links: osteoporosis, falls, knee OA (quads), vitamin D, decades · Tool: protein-needs estimator · Video: future

#### 2.3 Falls Prevention
- balance exercises (supported single-leg, heel-to-toe, weight shifts, **tai chi**); **home fall-proofing** (rugs, lighting, grab bars, footwear, mats); **medical review** (sedatives/BP drugs, vision, vitamin D, postural hypotension); empowering tone
- Cross-links: sarcopenia, osteoporosis, fragility fracture · Asset: **caregiver falls-prevention one-pager (PDF)**

#### 2.4 Vitamin D & Calcium *(India-relevant; cluster glue)*
- near-epidemic deficiency despite sunlight; **connective tissue between bone, muscle, falls**; food sources (veg-friendly); **test-and-treat**, individualised dosing; **caveats** (stone-formers, CKD, no self-megadosing/toxicity)
- Cross-links: every bone-muscle page, secondary osteoporosis, self-medication · Tool: calcium-intake estimator · Lab hook: Vitamin D Panel · Video: future

#### 2.5 Bone Health Across the Decades *(hub centrepiece)*
- the **"bone bank":** kids/teens (peak mass) → 20s–30s (maintain; female athlete triad) → 40s–50s (menopause acceleration; resistance + weight-bearing, vit D, baseline DEXA, steroid watch) → 60+ (preserve, falls, protein, screening); India threads (vit D, vegetarian Ca/protein)
- Cross-links: all bone-muscle pages

#### 2.6 Bone Health in Pregnancy & Lactation
- raised calcium/D demands; **pregnancy/lactation-associated osteoporosis** (rare, postpartum vertebral fractures); safe exercise; nutrition; when to seek help
- Cross-links: osteoporosis, vitamin D, decades · Lab hook: Antenatal Profile

#### 2.7 Secondary Osteoporosis — Conditions & Medicines
- **diseases:** CKD (CKD-MBD), hyperthyroidism, hyperparathyroidism, malabsorption/celiac, hypogonadism, diabetes, RA, Cushing's; **drugs:** glucocorticoids (steroid-induced — major), **long-term PPI**, anticonvulsants, aromatase inhibitors, GnRH agonists, heparin, excess thyroxine; identify/review the cause; screen at-risk
- Cross-links: osteoporosis, **self-medication (strong)**, AVN, vitamin D · Lab hook: bone profile, thyroid, PTH

#### 2.8 The First Fragility Fracture — A Warning
- low-trauma wrist/hip/spine fracture = **osteoporosis red flag**; "first fracture is a warning"; workup after first fracture; converts trauma patients into prevention
- Cross-links: osteoporosis, falls, decades

### PILLAR 3 — Children's Bone & Limb Health
> **Scoping:** reassurance-led (most physiological) + sharp "don't-miss" referral triggers; position as educator/early-detector/referrer, not over-claiming paediatric surgical scope. Start as one page; split DDH/CTEV later.

- **3.1 Children's legs & feet** — bowlegs (genu varum: physiological vs Blount's/rickets), knock knees (genu valgum), flatfoot (flexible vs rigid); reassurance + when to worry (asymmetry, pain, progression, short stature, persistence); rickets/vit-D link
- **3.2 DDH** — early detection critical; newborn checks, ultrasound <6mo; early Pavlik vs late; **refer early**
- **3.3 CTEV/clubfoot** — **Ponseti method**, early start, good outcomes; reassurance + referral
- Cross-links: vitamin D (rickets)

### STRAND A — Medication & Treatment Safety
**A.1 Self-Medication Risks: Painkillers & Steroid Abuse** *(high public-health value)*
- **Painkillers:** GI ulcers/bleeds, kidney injury (esp. CKD/HTN/elderly), CV risk, masking disease
- **Steroids:** unsupervised misuse (OTC/quacks/"instant relief"); harms — **steroid-induced osteoporosis, AVN**, cushingoid, worsened DM/BP, infection, adrenal suppression (never stop abruptly)
- **Message:** valuable *when prescribed and monitored*; danger is unsupervised/prolonged misuse
- **Tone rules:** empowerment + safety; not fear; not anti-doctor; no how-to; no naming/shaming
- Cross-links: AVN (strong), secondary osteoporosis (strong), knee OA (NSAID caution), metabolic strand

### STRAND B — Metabolic Health Awareness *(adjunct, educator-only — Dr. Agarwal does NOT treat these)*
> **Framing rule:** pure awareness/empowerment; every page states clearly that management belongs to the patient's treating physician. No treatment/dosing advice. Anchored to the MSK–metabolic connection where possible to stay topically tethered.

- **B.1 MSK–metabolic bridge pages** (woven into / linked from relevant ortho pages): how diabetes drives frozen shoulder, sarcopenia, poor fracture healing, foot problems; why metabolic control protects the musculoskeletal system
- **B.2 Flagship empowerment pilot:** *"'I'm taking a tablet' isn't enough — the monitoring every diabetic & hypertensive needs"* — complications awareness (CKD, heart, eyes, nerves, feet), why one normal reading ≠ control, the structured monitoring schedule, **plain-language HbA1c & lipid interpretation**, lifestyle relevance; firm "see your physician" framing
- **B.3 Action & Care Sheet (PDF):** a dedicated downloadable patient awareness/monitoring sheet to direct patients to or push via WhatsApp/EMR
- Cross-links: secondary osteoporosis, frozen shoulder, self-medication · **Lab hook (strong):** HbA1c, lipid, renal, diabetic/lipid panels → NK Pathology
- **Phase:** later; pilot first, expand only if it resonates and stays sustainable

## 8. Interactive Tools / Calculators

**Engagement + traffic + funnel layer.** Build as **bilingual, mobile-first, client-side HTML/JS widgets** (no data leaves the device), embedded in WordPress; reused across pages; housed in a **"Healthy Weight & Body Composition" tools hub**.

**Calculators:**
- **BMI** — on **Asian-Indian cut-offs** (overweight ≥23, obese ≥25; waist ≥90 cm M / ≥80 cm F), not Western
- **Waist-to-height ratio** — *the star*; "keep waist under half your height"; best cardiometabolic predictor
- **Waist circumference / waist-hip ratio** — India cut-offs
- **Body-fat estimate** — framed as estimate only; funnel to **BIA/DEXA at the clinic**
- **Protein-needs estimator** (g/kg) → links sarcopenia
- **Weight ↔ knee-load visualiser** → links knee OA (each kg lost = several kg less per-step knee load)
- **Calcium-intake estimator** → links osteoporosis/vitamin D

**Governance (critical):**
- Public tools = **educational/self-assessment only**. The nutrition/physio Excel's **comorbidity-gated prescriptive logic stays in-clinic** (behind "discuss with your doctor").
- **Design safety:** healthy *ranges* not single "ideal weight" targets; gentle, non-shaming language; no extreme deficits; never target underweight; always a "consult your doctor" line.
