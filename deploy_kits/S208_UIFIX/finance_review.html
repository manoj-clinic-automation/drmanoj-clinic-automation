Cash goes to the bank on the next trip, not at month end — so the drawer carries into the next month and finalising records what carried. Finalising also retires that month's scans to the connected Google Drive; the record of each file stays here forever, only the local copy goes.

Parked cash

—
A bank trip happens days after month end, so one deposit carries the closed month's parked cash plus this month's takings. Enter only the old month's share — the rest is this month's by definition. The deposit row itself is never split, so it keeps matching the bank statement exactly.

Count the drawer

The count is recorded as a fact; the gap between it and the computed ledger becomes one approved adjustment with your name on it. It does not close the legacy breaks — settling the balance and explaining the history are different things.
