It verifies the kit, copies the files, patches finance_app.py and portal.py ONLY if their pins are
the ones you type, restarts both services, then CHECKS they came back. If either did not, it
restores that file's backup by itself, restarts again, and prints the log. You end up with the
purchase pages live, or exactly where you started -- never at a 503.
