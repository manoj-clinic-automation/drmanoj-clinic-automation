Cash → UPI — record (Marg mein sudhaar nahin)

Ab Marg me sudhaar nahi karna hai — yeh record accountant ke liye rakha jata hai.
अब Marg में सुधार नहीं करना है — यह रिकॉर्ड accountant के लिए रखा जाता है। (CA का निर्णय, 13-Sep-2026)
Yeh bill bank ke hisaab se UPI se aaye, par Marg mein CASH likhe gaye. Inhe Marg mein badalna NAHIN hai — system yeh record rakhta hai aur mahine ke accountant report mein deta hai. Neeche sirf dekhne ke liye hai; koi kaam baaki nahin.

Ledger check and owner transfer

For one date: what the books actually hold, and the control to record a transfer that never landed. A transfer here does NOT move money -- it records who handed cash to whom, dated, with your name on it.
