S454: the screens as the owner saw them

A static mock, written by the Sanjeevni chat on 03-Oct-2026 and shown to the owner one screen at a time. On the staff screens (1 to 18) the words, the order and the buttons bind the build. On the owner screens (19, 20) the names of the states and the shape bind it. Suppliers and medicines are real, from Darpan's order sheet of 02-Oct; the counts, the masked phone numbers and the words "aa chuka" / "nahi aaya" on the old lines are examples. The colours and sizes were copied from the live porders.html. Nothing here is code to reuse. The brief lists, in its section 4.10, the screens this mock does not show.
