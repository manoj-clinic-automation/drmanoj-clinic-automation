S454_BILL_REGISTER: the screens as the owner saw them

A static mock, written by the Sanjeevni chat on 03-Oct-2026 and shown to the owner on a design canvas over several rounds. On the staff screens (1 to 12) the words, the order of the screens and the buttons bind the build. On the owner's register (13) the names of the states and the shape bind it. The suppliers, medicines, bill numbers and amounts are real ones read from the live pages; the counts are examples. The colours and sizes were copied from the live porders.html. Nothing here is code to reuse: it is the look and the wording. The brief lists, in its section 3.8, the screens this mock does not show.
