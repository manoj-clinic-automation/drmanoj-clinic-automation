S454_BILL_REGISTER: the screens as the owner saw them

A static mock, written by the Sanjeevni chat on 03-Oct-2026 and shown to the owner on a design canvas. He raised one point (two Yuvika estimate slips shown as bills not in Marg); they are out, and the group "Yeh kaunsa kagaz hai?" is the answer to it. The words, the order of the groups and the buttons are what he saw: build to them. The suppliers, bill numbers and amounts are of September as read on 02-Oct; the counts are worked out by hand and are examples. The staff pages keep their own live styles (porders.html); the colours here were copied from them.
