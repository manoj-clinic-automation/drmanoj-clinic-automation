## 0 · The ruling (D677) and why it fits the architecture

Darpan decides the order today by printing Marg's purchase-order sheet; the system captures it (S454, D666) and reception orders from it. The engine's own list (`order_proposal`, prepared 09:00 by `order_rules.tick`) is prepared every day and shown to nobody while `order.source = marg_sheet` — every row ends `merged`. D677 puts that list in front of Darpan on his own page, from 09:30, and makes his **Pakka** the hand-over to reception. Nothing new is computed: the lines are the engine's (S470, on the spine); the order book, the cards, the call / WhatsApp / arrival / scan flow are S454's, untouched; the Marg sheet road stays as the fallback, one setting away.

**What Darpan does** (three taps, nothing typed): hold a line (नहीं चाहिए / वापस लो), add a medicine from Marg's own item list (first letters, then a tap; its usual supplier and lot come from the spine's purchase lines; if it has none, a supplier chip), change a quantity by − / +, and **पक्का** per supplier (or सब पक्का). **What reception sees:** the Pakka'd supplier's lines on its own "Order karo" cards, exactly as a `system` proposal shows there today (± / remove / ordered / WhatsApp, `_mark_sources`). **What the owner sees:** `order.source` on his card reads `darpan`; his weekly score line keeps scoring the proposals (`order_rehearsal` reads `order_proposal` rows whatever their status).

## 2 · The tab, as built (the mock's behaviour, in Devanagari)

**The walk's scratch app:** `FINANCE_DB`, `FINANCE_PORT`, `FINANCE_ALLOW_HEADER_AUTH=1` with `X-Clinic-User` / `X-Clinic-Role` headers (finance_app.py ~l.40, 77, 135); `SPINE_DB` for a read-only copy of the live `spine.db` (the bundle has none) with the scratch folder carrying `spine/order_rules.json`; `ORDER_PUSH_STUB` for notices. Nothing live is opened for writing.

**Data.** `GET api/order` returns: `date`, `list_time`, `opened` (true from `order.darpan_list_time`), `source`, `suppliers[]` — each `{supplier_norm, vendor, display (the short name porders uses: Kedar, Shivaaz…), order_day (is_order_day today), status (open | darpan_ok | sent), pakka_at, lines[]}` — each line `{item, packing, unit (strip|unit), qty, on_hand_text (strips:tabs from on_hand and pack_size, e.g. 236 of 1*10 → "23:6"; a unit item → "11"), cover_days (the line's own field), held, added, why[]}`; plus `yesterday[]` — the previous order day's suppliers with reception's state read from `purchase_order` / `order_sheet` (ऑर्डर हो गया HH:MM · माल आया ✓ / अभी नहीं आया / फ़ोन नहीं उठा — रिसेप्शन फिर करेगी — take the words from what porders already shows for those states; invent none).

**Held and added lines live in the proposal's own `lines` JSON** (`held: true` on a held line — `entries()` and `send_proposal` must skip held lines: one condition each; an added line carries `added: true`, `vendor_norm`, and the engine's fields from `_s470_inputs(con, today)` (~l.1999: `(as_on, snap, pace, purch, transit, ortho)`; `purch[key]` gives `vendor, vendor_disp, rate_p, lot` — **`lot` is in units: `qty = lot ÷ pack_size`, rounded up to a strip**), `on_hand` from the snapshot — or `qty = 10 strips / 1 unit` and `why: ["Darpan ne joda"]` when the item was never bought). An added medicine whose supplier is not known is put under the supplier Darpan taps from chips (the suppliers that have a proposal today, then the other `order_supplier_rule` suppliers by name); an added medicine for a supplier with no proposal today creates a proposal row `kind='darpan'`, `status='open'` for it (UNIQUE(day, supplier_norm, kind) holds).

**Words — Devanagari, in the register of कल का हिसाब (short, polite, the page's own idioms):**
- tab: `आज का ऑर्डर` · header line: `सोम 05-10-2026 · 6 सप्लायर · 10 दवा` + `आज केदार का दिन` when a supplier's order day is today
- before the list time: `आज की सूची 9:30 बजे आएगी।` (the time from the setting) · no proposal today: `आज कोई ऑर्डर नहीं बनता।` · after all Pakka: `सब पक्का — रिसेप्शन ऑर्डर करेगी।`
- a supplier block (collapsible, like the page's `details`): name · `N दवा` · tag `आज का दिन`
- a line: item name + packing (as Marg prints them) · `शेल्फ 23:6 · ~4 दिन` (`खत्म` at 0) · quantity `20 पत्ता` / `11 नग` with − / + (step 10 strips at 20 or more, else 5; 1 for units; never below one step) · tap `नहीं चाहिए` → the line greyed, `इस बार नहीं`, tap `वापस लो` · an added line carries the tag `जोड़ी`
- per supplier: `पक्का` → `✓ पक्का — रिसेप्शन को गया HH:MM` and the block folds · bottom bar, fixed: `+ दवा जोड़ो` and `सब पक्का`
- the add sheet: title `दवा जोड़ो`, field placeholder `नाम के पहले 3 अक्षर`, hint `जैसे मार्ग में लिखते हैं`, each hit `name · packing · usual supplier · usual lot`, a hit with no supplier shows `सप्लायर चुनिए` and the chips; `बंद करो`
- folded at the foot: `कल के ऑर्डर — क्या हुआ (N)`
- **The strip word is `पत्ता` and the unit word `नग`** — the words `returns_desk.html` already uses after a count (`20 पत्ता`, `11 नग`); no other Hindi strip word exists on any staff page. Numbers in Latin digits as the rest of the page.

**Owner view** of the tab: the same list, no buttons, one line `Darpan ने पक्का किया HH:MM` per supplier (the owner's console is English elsewhere; this page is Darpan's and stays Hindi — the owner opens it as a reader).

**Phone and laptop, the same tab, the same flow** (the owner's rule of 03-Oct); nothing to type but the three letters of a name; no page reload on a tap (fetch, re-render the block).
