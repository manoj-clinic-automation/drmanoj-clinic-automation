## 3 · The edits

### 3.1 `stock_figure.py` — the start-of-day capture (D740; R2-B2, R2-B3)

- **Vocabulary.** A *start capture* is a `sp_close_name` capture printed day D and stamped on D between 09:00 and 22:00. It *may* stand for the end of D−1 (plus the morning's Marg work). Today `end_day` makes it None. **`end_day()` itself is not changed** (it is the clock rule, used by `covers()` for exports too). The change is in `captures()`: a start capture gets `end_day = D−1`, `usable = True`, `start = True` **only when its verdict is `accepted`**; `pending` or `set_aside` → `usable = False` as today. Every chooser (`capture_for`, `capture_at`, `closing`, `marg`, `shelf`, the proof) then uses it with no further change — and when two usable captures stand for the same `end_day` (yesterday's as-on print taken at 05:17 and today's start capture at 09:37), the **newest by stamp** wins, as `capture_for` already says: the morning's Marg work is in the later one.
- **The record: `stock_start_capture`** in `finance.db` (created by `stock_figure._ensure_start(con)` on first use): `md5 TEXT PRIMARY KEY, as_on, stamp_iso, end_day, verdict TEXT ('pending'|'accepted'|'set_aside'), reason TEXT, checked_at, built TEXT (sp_meta.built of the spine that judged), prev_md5, n_keys, n_excluded, n_unexplained, detail TEXT (JSON: the deciding keys, the excluded keys by cause)`. One row per md5, UPDATEd from `pending` to a final verdict once; a final verdict is a record and is not re-judged — the spine's nightly rebuild can move `bal()` under an accepted capture (aliases, late corrections); `built` records which spine judged, nothing acts on it: **named in the README as left**. The owner's switch `count.start_capture` (§3.4) off → `captures()` treats every start capture as unusable (the old rule) and writes nothing.
- **When a verdict can be given — `start_ready(con, sp, S, ctx)`:** the spine's `reach()` has `last_sale ≥ D` (the day's sale report is in — the next morning at the earliest: a start capture is never used on its own day) **and** the purchase side covers S by §3.2's rule (`purchase_cover`). Until then `pending`; **never `set_aside` for a short reach** (R2-B2).
- **The test — `verify_start(con, sp, S, ctx)`:** P = the newest *accepted or clean* usable capture with `end_day ≤ D−1` and `stamp < S.stamp` (the previous verified capture; none → `pending` with reason `no_prev`). For every key k (by `k20`) in S ∪ P:
  - `bal(k, d)` = Σ `sp_move.units` with `date ≤ d` (OPENING included — the spine's own stock at the end of d);
  - `off_S = units_S(k) − bal(k, D−1)`; `off_P = units_P(k) − bal(k, P.end_day)`; **`change = off_S − off_P`** (R2-B2: the change in the Marg-minus-spine offset, never an absolute compare — the spine never equals Marg item for item);
  - **excluded** (known non-spine Marg work between P and S, named in `detail` by cause): keys of count-voucher batches first entered in `(P.stamp_iso, S.stamp_iso]` (`stock_voucher_entered.at`, the newest non-empty row per batch, lines → keys through `key_of`); keys of `amir_job` `fix:%` rows with `done_at` in the window; `loss_piles.settings(con)["consume_keys"]`; keys with a PURCHASE dated D in `sp_move`; keys of a purchase bill with `purchase_bill.first_seen_day > P.stamp_iso[:10]` (a day, so a bill first seen on P's own day is undecidable — it is excluded too, conservatively); the `k20 == ""` bucket of `_cn_rows` is dropped (never a key);
  - **a sale before the capture:** an unexcluded key with `change < 0` and `|change| ≤ |Σ SALE units dated D|` (SALE units are **negative** in `sp_move`, spine_build 393 — the absolute sum) → `set_aside`, reason `sale_before`, the keys in `detail`;
  - **unexplained:** any other unexcluded key with `|change| ≥ 0.5`; more than `count.start_unexplained_max` of them → `set_aside`, reason `unexplained`; else `accepted` (the unexplained keys listed in `detail`, and §3.3's `approx` marks them on that capture).
- **Where it runs:** lazily, inside `captures()` when the context carries a **writable** `con` (every page read; memoised per `Ctx`; only the start captures of the last `LATE_DAYS` days with no final verdict are examined — a few rows), and by `python3 stock_figure.py verify-start` for the installer, the walk and the report. With no `con`, or a read-only connection (`gap_flag`'s `Ctx(con, None)` and the like), `captures()` only READS the verdict table (fail-soft: unreadable → the old rule) and writes nothing. No crontab line (the crontab is the parent's). Fail-soft: any exception → the capture stays `pending`, one stderr line.
- **R2-B3 — the same-day purchase on a start capture.** `marg()` on a *start* capture (`cap["start"]`, and the pre-09:00 case `early_bill` already names) adds over `(end_day, at]` only SALE / SALE_RETURN / RECONCILIATION dated D and every kind dated after D — never a PURCHASE / PURCHASE_RETURN dated D, which is already inside the capture when entered before it; such a key is `approx=True` with reason `purchase_on_start` **whenever the capture is a start capture, including `at == end_day`** (record_gaps skips only through `f["approx"]`, shelf_figure 374). Likewise a key the verification **excluded** that also has a SALE dated D is `approx` with reason `start_excluded` (its D-sale would otherwise be counted twice in the proof's `mv`). `shelf()`'s boundary, `record_gaps` / `rejudge` (shelf_figure, through `marg()` — the behaviour follows the one function), the voucher guard and the proof table skip those keys for judgment whether the start capture is the judged one or the base. `early_bill()` is folded into this: its one caller, the voucher guard (stock_app 5286), reads the `approx` reason instead; the name is retired (grep empty).
- **The owner's lines (`stock_watch.needs_you_lines`, stock_watch's file):** one English line per `set_aside` start capture of the last 7 days: *"Closing stock printed dd-mm hh:mm set aside — a sale came before it (ITEM, ITEM)"* / *"— N items changed with no Marg reason (ITEM, …)"*; and while `pending` for more than one day after D's sale report landed: *"Closing stock of dd-mm hh:mm waits for Amir's purchase export"*. ### 3.2 `stock_figure.py` — visit cover for the purchase side (R2-B4) and "agrees = right at once" (R2-B5)

- **`purchase_cover(con, sp, c, reach_, ctx)`** → True when the purchase side covers closing `c` (end_day d, read with `.get` — stock_app 8286 passes a bare dict) by any of:
  - **(a)** `reach_.last_pur ≥ d` and `covers(last_pur, pur_stamp, c, d)` (as today);
  - **(b)** the newest purchase export (both families, `sp_export` ok=1) is stamped after `c.stamp` (as today, the first clause of `covers`);
  - **(c) visit cover (R2-B4, restated after R1-B2):** let V be the Amir visit day (`amir_day.day`) on or before `c`'s calendar day with no later visit day before `c`; let E be a purchase export (both families; by anyone — `purchase_export` rows) stamped on or after V's **last** `amir_step_log` step-2 row (re-taps after the close included) and with no `amir_bill_answer` reason `self` and no open `amir_self_wait` after E's stamp. Then E covers every closing stamped after E and before the next visit day's first step-2 row. **On V itself the same clause applies** (R1-B3): an export stamped at or after V's newest step-2 row so far and before `c.stamp` covers `c`. A later export E2 (anyone's) whose `purchase_export.n_rows` and `grand_amount_p` equal E's (the owner's 16-10 13:03 = 15-10's 09:10) proves nothing was entered between them and is the cover from its own stamp. The facts this encodes (R2-B4): 15-10's 09:10 export is **not** cover on 15-10 after the re-taps 10:05 / 10:06, but the owner's identical 16-10 export covers 16-10 and 17-10; 18-10's 09:25 / 09:27 exports are not cover for the 09:37 closing (tap 09:35 after them) — that closing waits for the next export after 09:35; 04-10's 09:39 / 10:10 export, equal to the tap 10:10, covers the 10:22 closing.
- `tryable(c, reach_, con=None, ctx=None)` keeps its sale half and takes `purchase_cover` for its purchase half; with `con` None only (a) and (b) run. **Both callers pass `con`/`ctx`:** `stock_app._s524_tryable` (8663, and the bare-dict call at 8286), and **`shelf_figure.record_gaps` (341, `SF.tryable(c, R)`) — the kit's one edit to shelf_figure**, so the shelf-gap flags and the proof read one cover (charter 2). `short` stays `sale` | `pur`.
- **R2-B5:** in the proof table (§3.3) **every usable closing after the last entry is tried for `ok=True`**, whatever its purchase cover; `ok=False` is read only from the newest closing inside both reaches (N), as S528 left it. So an agreeing voucher is `proven` at once and a disagreeing one waits for cover before it is `wrong`.
