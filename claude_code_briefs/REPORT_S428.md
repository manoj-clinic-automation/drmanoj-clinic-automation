## For the owner
- **Full counts are Sundays only, every two months.** The last closed full count was 06-09-2026, so the next falls due from **06-11-2026** — **not due today**. When it falls due, Amir's board shows the next four Sundays as buttons ("Ginti ka Sunday chunein"); Darpan sees the picked date on his Stock milaan with "Theek hai" / "Nahi ho payega"; the confirmed date appears on your Needs you and on Shavez's month checklist. Two good counts in a row (loss under 1% of sales) move it to every 3 months by itself; one bad count (2% or more) moves it to monthly — each move is one line on your Needs you, and the setting stays yours.
- **Spot counts: three mornings a week (Mon, Wed, Sat), two items a morning.** A 06:30 job picks them from the sales, the arrivals, the last count's shortages, the item's value and how long since it was counted. **Tomorrow (Mon 28-09) Darpan gets: ONKET DT** (received 25-09, not counted since; short at the last count; sells 2 strips + 4 tabs a day) **and ZUPRINOR 5** (short at the last count; costly, Rs 659 a piece). He types patte + goli and taps "Bhej do"; each answer is a stock point. You can put any item at the head of his next list with **"Count this"** — on every line of your Loss desk and on the new **Counts & watch** card. Darpan can also count any item any day ("Stock batao") and flag a loss ("Kuchh gadbad hai").
- **Arrivals count as stock the moment the staff tap them in the purchase app** — provisional until Amir's bill reaches Marg; a tap that differs from the bill shows on your card, and a bill not entered within 3 days shows on Amir's board as "bill entry baaki".
- **When Darpan flags a loss, or a line sits in Big losses, the system traces it** from the last clean count to now through seven checks — the same bill entered twice, an arrival tapped but not entered, a pack-size mix-up, a twin item, scheme strips, a short delivery, a mistyped count — and says explained / partly / unexplained, with the fix as one line for Amir. Unexplained reaches your Needs you the same day.
- **The leakage budget is 1% of sales a month.** Your approvals page's Month section now carries "Leakage ₹X = Y% of sales · budget 1%" in green or red. **This month: Rs 0 = 0.0% — green** (the count of 06-09 is not closed yet, so nothing is written off). The watch list on your desk holds the 20 top sellers (TYRO BR, ROSIKA FORTE, BIO D3 MAX, ORICOX P, PATOPAN DSR …) and the costly items; each will show its last three counts. Every threshold is a setting under "Counts & watch". Installed and tested.
