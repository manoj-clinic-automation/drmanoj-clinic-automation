## PART B — `PB_MODEL` (D678)

### B.0 The shape
One setting **`order.model`** = `levels` | `fixed`. `fixed` is today's engine (the fallback; walk B.1 proves it line for line). `levels` changes six things inside the existing structure — the calendar, the gap, the rate, the level's floor, the rounding, the on-order window — makes the list after yesterday's sale report has landed, and adds three outputs (new item, supplier not known, count it). `plan()`'s shape, `prepare_day`, the proposals table and every owner limit (lead, safety, caps, single-source extra, blocked days, holidays, freeze, min line, min order, item rules, paused) stay. `purchase_app.plan_line` is **called, not edited**; `purchase_app.py`, `stock_watch.py`, `porders.py` are read only in Part B.

**B.0a The arithmetic is pure.** The model's sums live in eight functions in `order_rules.py` that touch no database and no clock — `model_rate(daily_units, end, long_days, short_days, short_min_sell_days, short_cap_pct)` → `(rate, rate_long, rate_short)` (the divisor is always the whole window) · `model_big_day(daily_units, end, days)` → `(units, date)` · `model_lot(purchase_lines, as_of, history_days)` → units or None · `model_interval(bill_dates, as_of, min_bill_days, default_days, lo, hi)` → `(interval, median_gap, bill_days)` · `model_level(rate, cover_days, big_day_units)` · `model_qty(need_units, pack_size, lot_units, on_hand_units, rate, cap_days, is_new)` → `(units, notes)`, or None when there is no lot (the caller then uses today's rails) · `model_interim_due(on_hand_units, rate, days_to_next_review, lead_days, margin_days)` · `model_cap_days(interval, cap_weekly, cap_fortnightly, cap_monthly)`. `plan()`, `interim_plan()` and `refresh_rhythm()` feed them from the spine, the bill register and the settings (`review_interval` applies the owner's `review_days` first, then `model_interval`'s rule to the stored median and bill days through the same code); the kit's `replay_s486.py` (walk B.9.7a) feeds the **same functions** from a nightly copy, day by day. What stays outside them, in both: today's rails for a line with no lot, the dead-item, minimum-line and minimum-order tests, and the calendar (the replay's calendar counts the policy's own orders — B.1's (a)/(b) in a world where every order passes through the system). ### B.2 The rate

### B.3 The quantity
`plan_line` returns `order_strips` already rounded to a box (PA:1225–1231) and decides *listed or not* (need, dead item, minimum line) — that decision stands. In `plan` straight after it and **before** the keep and max-shelf rails (OR:812–825) and the min-line guard (OR:828), which then act on the result as today — on `levels`, for a line it lists:
- **The level has a floor, for an item with a rate above zero:** `level = max(rate × min(cov, pa.MAX_COVER_DAYS), big_day)` — the shelf never plans for less than the largest day's sale of the last `order.big_day_days` days (one long prescription). Replay: 110 → 100 empty-shelf days at the same stock and the same number of orders. `plan_line` knows no floor and returns its whole dict in every case (PA:1252–1258): a line it leaves at `order_strips ≤ 0` **with an empty `why`** (not needed) is listed by `plan` when `it["on_hand"] < big_day`, unless `it["days_since_sale"] > dead_after` (`plan_line` tests the dead item only when it has a quantity, PA:1245 — `plan` tests it here); a line `plan_line` zeroed for the dead item (PA:1247) or the minimum line (PA:1250) stays zeroed. The line's `why` gains "kept for one large sale (N on dd-mm)"; the keep / max-shelf rails and the minimum-line guard (OR:828) then act on it as on any line.
- `need_units = max(0, level − it["on_hand"])` (`it["on_hand"]` includes arrivals, OR:805); `lot_strips = max(1, round(lot ÷ pack_size))` (`lot` is units: `_s470_purch`, OR:1961/1987).
- **With a lot in the window:** `qty = ceil(need_units ÷ pack_size ÷ lot_strips) × lot_strips`, at least one lot; then the cap as a limit — the largest multiple of the lot with `(it["on_hand"] + qty × pack_size) ÷ rate ≤` the cover cap, never below one lot; one lot above the cap sets `confirm` and adds `why` "one lot is N days of sale". The line carries `lot_based: true`; `_line_out` (OR:746/749, reached through the wrapper OR:2093) does **not** apply `_staff_qty` to it. `plan_line`'s own `why` and `confirm` entries are kept as they are; ours are appended ("usual lot 10 strips: 20 instead of 14").
- **No lot in the window:** today's rails (box, 10-then-tens), unchanged; for a floor-listed line with no lot the quantity is `_staff_qty(ceil(need_units ÷ pack_size), unit)`. Carried shorts (OR:840–861) unchanged.
- **Scheme:** `free` (a ratio, OR:1988) > 0 → `why` gains "scheme 10 + 1"; `cover_after` counts the free units; the ordered quantity is the bought quantity.
- **New item, when listed:** one lot (or the rails' minimum with no lot), `why` "new medicine (first seen dd-mm) — one lot". A new item with no need is not listed (OR:826 as today).
